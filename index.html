Features & Modules

Explore what makes BTTUNE the ultimate music streaming client.

stream

Streaming & Playback

Ad-Free Low-Latency Audio
  • Ad-Free Engine: Stream millions of tracks, albums, and playlists without interruption.
  • Background Playback: Native foreground service ensuring continuous playback during screen-off and multitasking.
  • Dynamic Autoplay: Machine-learning recommendations that automatically queue related tracks.
  • Smart Audio Cache: Streamed audio blocks are cached locally to minimize network data usage on replay.

Interface & Themes

Pure Jetpack Compose & AMOLED
  • Dynamic Color Harmony: Adaptive Material You color extraction from system wallpaper and album artwork.
  • Custom Player Canvases: Switch between Pure AMOLED Black, Liquid Glass, Neon Glow, and Vinyl turntable views.
  • System Integration: Rich MediaStyle notification actions, lock screen playback controls, and Always-On Display (AOD).

Lyrics & Vocal Sync

Realtime Word-by-Word Karaoke
  • Syllable-Level Tracking: Time-accurate word-by-word karaoke highlighting in real time.
  • Multi-Source Fallback: Automatic aggregation across global lyric repositories (LRCLIB, Musixmatch, BetterLyrics).
  • AI Translation & Romanization: In-line pronunciation guides and multi-language lyrics translation.
  • Lyric Canvas Export: Generate shareable typography lyrics cards for social media.
spatial_audio

Studio DSP & Soundstage

8D Surround & Parametric EQ
  • 8D & Spatial Soundstage: Virtual surround sound mapping tailored for immersive headphone listening.
  • Parametric Equalizer: Multi-band frequency adjustment with custom acoustic presets and bass boost.
  • Gapless DJ Automix: Configurable crossfade handoff curves for seamless transitions between tracks.
  • USB Bit-Perfect Bypass: Direct driver bypass engineered for external audiophile USB DAC hardware.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BTTUNE.

Yes. BTTUNE is 100% open-source under GPL 3.0. It contains zero ads, zero subscriptions, and zero tracking code.

BTTUNE enforces a strict zero telemetry policy. No third-party analytical SDKs or marketing trackers exist in the codebase. All your library data remains encrypted locally on your device.

BTTUNE leverages Google's Media3 / ExoPlayer pipeline coupled with custom 8D and spatial DSP processors, a parametric equalizer, gapless DJ crossfading, and USB bit-perfect direct output for external audiophile DACs.

Lyrics are aggregated with real-time multi-source fallback across global repositories including LRCLIB, Musixmatch, and BetterLyrics, supporting word-by-word syllable karaoke highlighting and AI translation.

Any Android device running Android 8.0 Oreo (API level 26) or higher with at least 1GB RAM.
